# NeurIPS 2026 response-period analyses

These files archive the analyses produced for the BatteryMat NeurIPS 2026 submission during the author response period (July to August 2026): a blind prospective DFT validation campaign, training-set disclosure checks, and the exact data partition record for the tier-1 ALIGNN voltage surrogate.

## Prospective DFT validation campaign

Twelve materials with no prior DFT voltage data were selected before any DFT was run: the 8 top-ranked candidates from the screening pool and 4 low-ranked controls. Each ran through the automated tier-3 protocol (`../dft_prep.py`: PBE+U or optB88-vdW+U by framework, in-house Li-metal reference, sequential supercell delithiation with ALIGNN-FF vacancy ranking). All 12 chains completed.

Headline result: complete separation. Every top candidate has a DFT average voltage of 3.40 V or higher and every control sits at 1.35 V or lower, with no overlap. Tier-2 (ALIGNN-FF) MAE against DFT is 0.88 V over the 12 materials. The two Rb2LiFeF6 polymorphs, run independently, agree to 2 mV.

Files:

- `prospective_table.md` is the final results table, regenerated by `04_prospective_table.py`.
- `prospective_selection.csv` is the pre-registered selection with tier-2 predictions, produced by `03_select_prospective.py`. Note that rerunning the selection now excludes the campaign materials themselves, since they sit in `../dft_inputs/` as completed runs. The archived CSV is the original pre-registered selection.
- The per-step DFT evidence lives in `../dft_inputs/<JID>/supercell_*/` alongside the five commercial benchmark cathodes: `energies.json` per material and voltage and discharge curve PNGs for the completed chains. Raw VASP outputs stay out of git per the repository convention. The OSZICARs used to recover the two lagged recordings (see note below) live in the campaign archive alongside the full step directories on the cluster.
- The SLURM chain automation is in `../`: `chain_step.sh` (one self-resubmitting link per VASP relaxation, strictly serial per material), `launch_prospective.sh` (fleet initialization), and `check_chains.sh` (status and running voltages).

Known issue: the per-step JSON recording can stop updating mid-chain while the DFT steps continue correctly. The recovery is to read the final E0 from each step's OSZICAR and fill the missing entries. Recorded values are smeared free energies and OSZICAR E0 is the sigma-to-zero value, so differences below 0.04 eV are expected.

## Training partition record

The tier-1 ALIGNN voltage surrogate was trained on a labeled pool of 7,610 structures with a fixed-seed 80/10/10 uniform random split (6,088 train, 761 validation, 761 test, seed 123). The exact ID lists and the full training configuration (alignn_atomwise, CGCNN atom features, 8 Angstrom cutoff, 12 neighbors, 250 epochs, batch size 32, learning rate 0.001) are archived in `../../average_voltage/Li_250_ids_train_val_test.json` and `../../average_voltage/Li_250_train_config.json`, next to the voltage label data the split partitions. Held-out test MAE is 0.167 V over 761 structures against a label mean absolute deviation of 1.244 V.
